Slab rules and how it is calculated

Review % = reviews ÷ Flipkart India / Cloudtail India installation tickets closed in the current month. Every review ticket is compared with the closed installation tickets of all months in the calls report, and each match counts as a review; only the current month's closed tickets form the denominator. It is worked out separately for each category. Earning = reward per review of the slab reached × all reviews of that category. Industrial products (IND, KF) are not counted.

Review dashboard: uploaded, accepted (counted as reviews) and rejected tickets, franchise review %, technician slab, earnings and reviews needed for the next slab. Rejected tickets: review tickets that could not be counted (cancelled call, other dealer, not in the calls report). If all uploaded tickets are accepted: the case where every uploaded review ticket, including the rejected ones, is accepted.
